Nobody’s Witness · Original Uncut · Depth Maps

Eleven real depth maps, voted and installed.

Recreated Original Uncut stills that had no 2.5D camera now each have a hand-directed greyscale depth map drawn from their own plate. Every map was voted between two candidates; the accepted one is shown left, its plate right. Nothing was blurred or faked: near-white is close to camera, near-black is far, and every silhouette edge is traced from the frame. Two further targets — Ishida’s answer and Jack over Ishida — were rejected and are recorded below with their prompts.
