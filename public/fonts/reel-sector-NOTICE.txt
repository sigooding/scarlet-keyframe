Reel Sector
===========

reel-sector.woff2 is an original bitmap display font drawn for Scarlet Reel.
Its glyph outlines are generated from the hand-authored 5x7 patterns in
scripts/hollywood/prepare-fidelity-v6.py. It contains no extracted Duke Nukem
font data. It is provided under the same terms as this repository's source.
The separately bundled Oswald font retains its own existing OFL license.
