## Car / seat lock (the consistency rule)

- **Car:** black 1990s Toyota Crown executive sedan, chrome grille, silver
  hubcaps, beige/tan leather interior, warm amber dome light.
- **Exteriors (01–04):** car nose always points **screen-LEFT**, police station
  entrance always behind it, camera always on the road side.
- **Kurose's seat:** always the **far-side rear seat** in every frame he
  appears in (01 silhouette in the glass, 02 silhouette in the lit gap, 03
  full reveal, 05 offering tea across the bench). The near-side seat stays
  empty for Ishida.
- **Interiors from the front (06):** Kurose always **screen-LEFT**, Ishida
  always **screen-RIGHT** with the cup. (Deliberately mirrored from the
  original `141-he-drinks.jpg` so the seat matches the exteriors.)
- **Kurose:** 70s, silver slicked-back hair, navy 3-piece suit, silver watch
  with white dial + brown strap on left wrist (matches `s51/311` and `s83`).
- **Ishida:** late 40s, greying black hair, grey shirt + dark blazer, soaked.

## Dialogue notes (s94 — replaces "It's only tea.")

Director's brief: this is the last time we see Ishida alive. He is going to
be taken out, but it must not be obvious. Kurose talks around the development
and nothing standing in its way.

### Recommended lines

The tea offer plays as **silent action** (cup held out on 05, taken — no
words, scarier). Then two short Kurose lines across the boarding beats:

> **Over 05 (the cup held out):**
> **KUROSE** *(gently)*: You are wet, Ishida. Take this. It will warm
> your hands.
>
> **Over 06 (doors closed, rain suddenly far away):**
> **KUROSE** *(evenly, looking ahead)*: The plaza is finished now. Do you
> know what takes the longest? Not the building. The clearing. One removes
> a branch here, a stone there… until nothing stands in the way of the light.
>
> **ISHIDA**: *(silence — he drinks)*

Why:
- **Deniable.** First watch: a chairman musing about landscaping. Rewatch
  (after s96, "He left a note"): "the clearing" and "nothing in the way"
  land as the sentence being passed. No explicit threat.
- **His voice.** Matches s83 ("Good men are very expensive") — aesthetic,
  architectural, never hurries, never raises it.
- **Ishida's silence is the performance.** He doesn't ask where they're
  going. He drinks the tea. He already knows — more frightening than any
  protest, and it honours his last scene alive.

### Alternative B — "The note" (dramatic irony aimed at s96)

> **KUROSE**: You look tired. A man in your position should write things
> down, Ishida. So there can be no… confusion, later.

Ice cold once the audience learns he "left a note," but slightly more
pointed than the recommendation.

### Alternative C — minimalist (one line, tied to the lit window)

As Ishida looks back at the station:

> **KUROSE**: Don't look back. They have all gone home.

Then nothing but the door closing. Shortest, bleakest.
