# s2_new2 — Scene 2, “The small bar”

A fresh, self-contained reshoot of Scene 2 for *Nobody's Witness*. Everything is saved in this folder; no original scene art, `s1_new`, `s2_new`, film data, or depth maps were replaced or wired into the reel.

## Visual and continuity decisions

- **New bar master:** `style-sheets/location-current-bar.jpg` is the location source of truth for this set. It was built from the current `s2_new2` frames. Do not use the old Scene 2 bar as a location reference; its layout was wrong for this pass.
- **Orientation:** looking inward from the street entrance, the counter and bottle shelves are on frame-left and the rain windows on frame-right. A reverse view facing the entrance swaps those sides. The room itself is never mirrored.
- **Fixed journalist position:** he stays in the same place at the counter, identified by the brown notebook, black pen and beer glass. He remains seated through shot 25 and is still seated at first contact in shot 26.
- **Shot 20 repeat:** `20-the-journalist-v2.jpg` is the current version and was generated without specifying seating furniture or a furniture count. The earlier `20-the-journalist.jpg` is preserved unchanged as an alternate; it is not used in the current contact sheet.
- **Original Mara look:** Mara's face and clothes follow the original Scene 1 references (`../s1/03-mara-walks.jpg` and `../s1/04-mara-phone.jpg`), not the newer `s1_new` / `s2_new` styles.
- **Clear action:** Mara sees him through the glass, rushes in, notices the approaching men and hides under the near entrance-end of the customer-side counter before they cross the threshold. The journalist is grabbed at 26. The shots are implied off-screen; the men leave him on the floor beside his overturned seat. No firing, wounds or blood are shown.
- **Silencer visible:** a lowered pistol with its cylindrical silencer is visible in shots 25–27; shot 26 gives the clearest view.
- **Mara's reaction:** after the quiet aftermath in 28, she emerges just enough to see the journalist and covers her mouth with both hands in fear in the new `29-mara-gasps.jpg`.
- **No till/cash register** in this set.
