## Geography and camera orientation

The bar is one narrow, fixed room. Its layout does not change between angles:

- Looking **inward from the street entrance**: the counter, stools and amber bottle shelves are on frame-left; rain-streaked shopfront windows are on frame-right. This is the orientation for 20, 21, 24, 25 and 26.
- Looking **back toward the entrance** from inside: the windows are on frame-left and the counter/shelves on frame-right. This is the reverse view for 22, 27, 28 and 29.
- Shot 19 is viewed from outside through the rain glass. Shots 23 and 308 are low customer-side views under the near entrance-end of the counter.
- The counter and shopfront must not swap sides in two shots facing the same direction. Do not mirror the room to solve a framing problem.

## Fixed positions and action

- The journalist remains in the same place at the counter, marked by his brown notebook, pen and beer glass. Do not move him between cuts.
- He stays seated through shot 25 and remains seated at the instant of first contact in shot 26. The shots are implied off-screen between 26 and 27; in 27 he is down on the floor beside the overturned furniture. He remains there in 28 and 29 after the gunmen leave.
- Mara sees him through the glass, enters, then hides beneath the **near entrance-end, customer side** of the counter. She is not behind the serving counter or in a back-room crate area.
- The two men are outside while she hides in 23 / 308; they cross the same street threshold in 24. They approach without touching him in 25, grab him in 26, then leave in 27.
- The bird clip falls from Mara's wet hair during the hide and is visible on the floor in 308. In 29, after the men leave, she sees the journalist and covers her mouth with both hands in fear.
- The notebook, pen and beer stay at the journalist's place on the counter after he falls. No till/cash register is included.

## Cast and weapon continuity

**Mara Voss, 24:** original fair-skinned blonde woman; wet shoulder-length hair; red enamel bird clip at her left temple until it slips off; dark indigo denim jacket, grey tee, black jeans, white canvas trainers; small brass key. No wardrobe or hairstyle change. In 29 she is frightened, gasping, with both hands over her mouth; the clip is off her hair.

**Journalist, 40s:** Japanese man with short receding dark hair, wire-rim glasses, charcoal suit and pale blue open-collar shirt.

**Gunmen:** two masked men in dark rain-soaked coats, gloves and boots. A compact pistol with a clearly visible cylindrical silencer is carried low in shots 25–27; it is not fired on-screen. No blood or graphic injury is shown.
