# The Hive Escape (scenes 85–92) — continuity lock

Fresh set for *Nobody's Witness* on the Scarlet Reel. **Nothing in the shipped film is replaced:**
`public/film/img/s85/`–`s92/`, `src/film/story.json` and `src/film/studio-edits.json` are untouched.
All new work lives in `public/film/img/thehiveescape/`. Loosely following the script — the visual
logic is: **Kaneko gives the signal on the pipe → the whole Hive passes it on and helps → Vera and
Jack escape through other people's rooms → out the rear window, down the ladder under a train.**

## Look

Photorealistic contemporary Tokyo neo-noir, 16:9 film stills. Cold slate/blue-green shadows, warm
amber practicals only, wet surfaces, steady rain outside, restrained 35mm grain. No legible signage
or text in frame. No sound-wave graphics on the pipe shots — the signal is hands, spanners, spoons.

## Geography of the escape (does not change between cuts)

Kaneko's counter (86, signal) → back doorway → passages (85, relay + masked men; 88, blackout) →
stairwell by the dentist (89, up by touch) → through the rooms (90: old woman → family kitchen →
dentist → violin girl's bedroom → wardrobe passage → barber → laundry → shrine hatch → plank
between balconies → pipe gallery) → last empty rear room, window out (91) → iron ladder down the
rear wall under the passing train → street below the viaduct (92), Hive lit up again behind them.

Screen direction: Vera leads, Jack follows her sound/hand, through s88–s90. Residents never speak,
never look at camera; each one opens, holds, points, parts or steadies something, then closes the
way behind them. Masked men are always a corridor or a floor behind, never in the same room.
