# thehiveescape — scenes 85–92, fresh set

A new, self-contained set of images for the Hive escape (scenes 85–92) of *Nobody's Witness*.
Nothing was replaced: the film still plays the pictures in `public/film/img/s85/`–`s92/`.
Loosely scripted — the cut is built so it reads visually: Kaneko strikes the pipe, the building
answers floor by floor, every resident helps without a word, and Vera and Jack pass through the
Hive's rooms and out under a train. Character locks are in `style-sheets/` (see
`style-sheets/continuity.md`); pass the relevant sheet with any new or replacement shot.

## Format / before swapping in

All frames and sheets here are conformed to the house format (1920×1080 sRGB JPEG, quality 92,
Lanczos cover-crop + light unsharp from the 1376×768 generator output), as `s1_new` did.
`contact-sheet.jpg` shows all 33 story shots in order and is a review aid only.
If you swap any shot into the film, remake its depth map from the new picture — never reuse the
old shots' maps.

## Pace note (director's notes, this pass)

The escape is played at *believable* urgency: fast purposeful movement, natural strides, coats
moving with the body, people breathing hard — not sprint-posing. Scene 90's middle shots lean
hardest into the rush (a beat ahead of the men); Kaneko in 86 and the unlit-lighter pause in 89
stay still on purpose. From 90-10 on, Vera was generated with her original character sheet
(`style-sheets/vera.jpg`) as the lead reference in every frame.
