# roadside — the roadside inn, scenes 30–49, fresh set

A new, self-contained set of images for the **roadside inn** sequence of *Nobody's Witness*
(scenes 30–49: the arrival, the night, and **Jack escaping through the inn**).

**Nothing was replaced.** The film still plays the pictures in `public/film/img/s30/`–`s49/`;
`story.json` and `studio-edits.json` are untouched. Everything new lives here.

Loosely scripted: where the screenplay's staging fights the building, the staging was re-drawn so
the route is geometrically true — out of the upstairs bath window onto the kitchen roof, down the
gutter, in through the open back door, forward to the lobby for Mr. Noda, back to the kitchen, out
to the yard, away in the pickup. Character and location locks are in `style-sheets/`
(see `style-sheets/continuity.md`); pass the relevant sheet with any new shot.

Scene **36** (Vera's apartment) is out of scope — it is not the roadside inn.

**Status: complete.** 9 style sheets and **53 story shots** across scenes 30–49.
`contact-sheet.jpg` shows all of them in order.

## Format / before swapping in

All frames and sheets here are conformed to the house format (1920×1080 sRGB JPEG, quality 92,
Lanczos cover-crop + light unsharp from the 1376×768 generator output), the same treatment
`s1_new` and `thehiveescape` got. `contact-sheet.jpg` shows the story shots in order and is a
review aid only. If you swap any shot into the film, remake its depth map from the new picture —
never reuse the old shots' maps.
