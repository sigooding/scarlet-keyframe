# Mara's Service (scene 97A) — service_new — continuity lock

Fresh set for *Nobody's Witness* on the Scarlet Reel. **Nothing in the shipped film is
replaced:** the film still plays the fourteen pictures in `public/film/img/s97a/` (PlayFrame
shots 556–569), `src/film/story.json` and `src/film/studio-edits.json` are untouched, and
`art/mara-service/` keeps the director's concept stills. All new work lives in
`public/film/img/service_new/`, using **the same fourteen shot numbers and filenames** as
`s97a/` so any picture can be dropped into the cut one-for-one.

Character consistency is taken **from the original shipped scenes only** — the `s97a/`
frames and the other `_new` sets were **not** used as identity references.

## Character locks (pass the sheet with every shot that uses the person)

- **Vera** (`../../sheets/vera-face.jpg` — face authority, as in the Hive-escape set):
  early 30s American, fair, blue-grey eyes, blonde hair pinned in a loose low bun with loose
  strands at the forehead. Service wardrobe: **long charcoal-black wool coat over a white
  shirt and pale grey sweater, black trousers, black shoes.** She owns and carries the
  **pale blue umbrella** — the only blue one in the film.
- **Vera's mother** (`style-sheets/vera-mother.jpg` — this set's new sheet; the shipped film
  has no other frame of her): early 60s American, Vera's mother — same family bone
  structure and blue-grey eyes, two decades older. **Silver-blonde shoulder-length hair,
  loosely pinned; long black wool coat over a plain black crew-neck dress; small pearl stud
  earrings; thin gold wedding band.** Dignified, contained grief; she does not weep in
  front of Vera.
- **Jack** (`../../thehiveescape/style-sheets/jack.jpg` — face authority): late 50s
  American, tall and lean, lined stubbled face, dark swept-back hair greying at the sides.
  Service wardrobe: **long dark wool overcoat over a white shirt with a black tie, dark
  trousers.** The only time he wears a tie in the film.
- **Kaneko** (`../../thehiveescape/style-sheets/kaneko-and-counter.jpg` — face authority):
  late 70s Japanese, small, straight grey hair in a low bun. Service wardrobe: **black
  formal kimono-style jacket over a dark dress** — her own room, dressed for her lodger's
  memorial. This is her room; she is **present in every room shot** (556 is the only room
  shot she is not in, and she is not in the room in it).
- **Okada** (`../../s81/100-the-bar-in-daylight.jpg` — face authority): 60s Japanese, the
  bar owner from Kanda; short silver hair, weathered still face. **Black suit, white shirt,
  black tie**, late, never comes in, umbrella planted like a cane.
- **Mara** — alive only as the **memorial photograph** (`style-sheets/mara-memorial-photo.jpg`;
  face authority `../../s2/21-mara-bursts-in.jpg` and `../../s69/279-her-sisters-smile.jpg`):
  early twenties American, soft round young face, large blue-grey eyes, long wavy
  golden-blonde hair, warm genuine smile, dark top; portrait orientation in a **simple
  light-wood frame**. The photo is **the same object with the same face in every shot it
  appears in** — it never changes. Her drawings are pencil sketches on white sketch paper.
- **The three Hive neighbours** (`style-sheets/service-neighbours.jpg`, left to right):
  an elderly man in his 80s with a dark walking cane (straight white-grey hair, long dark
  suit, black tie); a stockier man around 70 (short grey hair, dark suit, black tie, hands
  folded); a small woman in her 70s (grey hair in a low knot, long dark grey dress, small
  dark shawl, hands clasped). Ordinary people; they knew Mara from the corridors.

## The room (does not change between cuts — `style-sheets/loc-service-room.jpg`)

Kaneko's back room in the Hive: tatami; **low wooden table at centre**; on it, left to
right, **vase of white chrysanthemums — incense bowl with one stick going straight up —
Mara's framed photograph — cup of tea — her drawings dried flat**. LEFT wall: dark chest
of drawers with the small lamp (the room's warm practical). BACK wall: big window, pale
lace curtains, the neighbouring building close beyond in the rain. RIGHT corner: curved
shoji screen and a dark wooden cupboard. Entry door right foreground, wooden step down,
**umbrella stand just inside** with the folded black umbrellas and the pale blue one.
Six grey cushions: **three on the left for Vera (nearest the door), her mother, Jack;
three on the right for the three neighbours; Kaneko kneels at the foot of the table,
nearest the door, host's place.** Seven people, always the same seven.

## The lane (does not change between cuts — `style-sheets/loc-lane-and-door.jpg`)

One doorway on the **LEFT wall** near the camera: worn step up, deep eave, paper notice,
umbrella stand under the eave. The lane runs away from the door; **the concrete railway
viaduct crosses over its far end.** Rain throughout; the last of the light for the arrival
(556–560), properly dusk-dark by the time they come out (569).

## Staging fixes over the shipped set (why these pictures differ)

1. **561** — Kaneko was missing from her own room and an unexplained seventh man stood at
   the window; the memorial photo showed a stranger. Now: the seven people locked above,
   Mara's photo, nothing else in the room.
2. **562** — the parcel was a cloth bundle. The script says newspaper (scene 68: she gave
   Mara rice balls wrapped in newspaper for the car). Now: **newspaper, string-tied.**
3. **563** — the photo frame and the "arches in the rain" drawing now match 561/562; the
   drawing is consistent: a brick railway arch with rain hatching, tracks through it.
4. **564** — the overhead now matches 561's seating (Kaneko was at the foot in 564 only,
   before; she is at the foot in both now).
5. **565** — the bow lost the mother and Kaneko in the shipped frame. All seven are in
   frame now: Vera and Jack bow at the table, the rest kneel bowed.
6. **569** — the script's "her mother stays behind with the photograph a while longer" had
   no picture. The shot now holds the door open behind the walkers: the mother visible in
   the lit doorway, the viaduct and passing train over the far end of the lane, Vera's
   blue umbrella the only blue.

## How to reference (house rule, as in `roadside/` and `thehiveescape/`)

Original shipped frames are used **sparingly, for identity only** — faces and the two
shells (room, lane), never as compositions to copy. At most one or two shipped frames in a
call, and only where a face or a shell has to match. The style sheets of this set are the
composition and staging authority.

## Look

Photorealistic contemporary Japanese neo-noir, 16:9 film stills. Cold slate / teal-blue-green
shadows and rain, warm amber practicals only, wet reflective surfaces outside, restrained
35mm grain, natural exposure, subtle highlight halation. **No film grade, no volumetric
light shafts, no legible signage or text, no logos, number plates, watermarks or borders.**
