# Jack and Ishida in the car: new set (scene 61, "Nine o'clock")

This redoes the images for **scene 61, INT. ISHIDA'S CAR – NIGHT**, the only scene with Jack and
Ishida in a car. Nothing in `s61/` or anywhere else was touched. This folder is a standalone set of
images and notes only: no `story.json`, studio-edits or code changes.

The script is followed loosely. Every scripted moment that matters gets its own picture, and where
the script or the old pictures broke the geometry, they were fixed (see *Fixes vs the originals*).

**Status:** complete — 11 of 11 shots plus all the style sheets, on `contact-sheet.jpg`.

## Car / seat lock (the consistency rule)

See `style-sheets/car-ishidas-saloon.jpg` (the car) and `style-sheets/car-seating-plan.jpg` (who
sits where, plus the camera map).

- **Car:** dark bottle-green metallic 1990s Japanese saloon, **right-hand drive**. It has:
  - mouse-grey velour seats and a dark grey dash;
  - green-backlit gauges behind the wheel, plus a green clock and a radio-cassette on the centre
    stack;
  - a floor selector;
  - a plain mirror with nothing hanging from it, and nothing personal anywhere.

  It is deliberately unlike every other car in the film: Jack's navy saloon, Vera's maroon
  hatchback, the grey trap sedan (67/70) and the black sedans of Kurose and the masked men.
- **ISHIDA always drives from the RIGHT front seat**, behind the wheel. **JACK is always in the LEFT
  front seat**, with no wheel in front of him. The rear seats are empty.
- **From the front, Ishida is always screen-LEFT and Jack screen-RIGHT.**
  - Ishida looks screen-right to Jack; Jack looks screen-left to Ishida.
  - Cameras stay on the windscreen side of the line between them.
  - The only shots on the line itself are each man's view of the other (03, 04), Jack's look at the
    hands (09) and the view in through Jack's window (11).
  - There are no back-seat angles.
- **Exterior:** parked at the **left kerb** under the expressway (Japan keeps left). The camera is
  on the road side and the nose points **screen-RIGHT**. The car sits under the deck's outer edge, so
  run-off drums on the roof and rain gathers on the windscreen.

## Characters (locked to the shipped film)

- **Ishida** (`style-sheets/ishida.jpg`): Japanese detective, mid-50s.
  - Short, neatly combed salt-and-pepper hair; clean-shaven.
  - Charcoal herringbone tweed jacket and a pale grey-blue shirt open at the collar, with no tie.
  - Face authority: shipped frames `s23/441`, `s80/99`, `s7/66`.
- **Jack** (`style-sheets/jack.jpg`): late 50s, lean.
  - Swept-back dark hair greying at the sides; grey stubble.
  - Long charcoal-black wool overcoat over an off-white open shirt.
  - No plaster on the inn cut, matching the shipped 59, 62 and 63.
  - Face authority: shipped frames `s23/351`, `s59/477`, `s80/95`.

## Fixes vs the originals (visual-sense changes)

- **Right-hand drive.** Every shipped s61 frame is left-hand drive, which is wrong for Tokyo. The
  originals also disagree with each other: `481`–`484` have Ishida driving from the left, and `228`
  has **Jack** behind the wheel. Here Ishida always drives from the right seat.
- **"Neither looks at the other."** In `228` the two men look at each other. Here they don't, until
  the script turns them: Jack in 06 and 08, Ishida once in 10.
- **Rain on a car under a roof.** The originals park fully under the deck yet show rain on the glass.
  Here the car sits under the deck's outer edge, where the run-off is visible, which justifies
  "rain drumming on the roof" and "eyes on the rain on the windscreen".
- **Jack's hair.** It changed between the originals (long and dark in `228`/`483`, grey and swept
  back in `482`). It's now locked to the rest of the film via the face plate.
- **Jack's eyes.** `484` was framed over Ishida's own shoulder. 09 is what Jack sees, from his seat.
- **One screen geography.** No shot flips the men. The camera map is on `car-seating-plan.jpg`.
- **The establishing wide.** At that size the generator kept giving the passenger the wrong face, so
  the two men in 01 are kept dim in the cabin: readable as two different men (a Western passenger, a
  Japanese driver) but not fully resolved, with the green dash glow and raindrops kept. Faces are
  first read in 02.

## Before swapping these in

- **Filenames.** They are new and unique (`61-NN-…`), so the bare-filename lookups in
  `scripts/film-depth.entry.ts`, `tools/depth-parallax-probe.py` and `tools/review-depth-maps.py`
  can't collide with `s61/`.
- **Light glows.** `228-nine-oclock` has three `pictureLook` light glows in `studio-edits.json`
  (dash teal at 36/64 and 49/78, amber at 50/37), placed for the old frame. Re-place them on
  whichever new frame opens the scene, or drop them.
- **Depth maps.** No s61 picture has one today. `docs/film-depth-layers.json` lists
  `228-nine-oclock` as a people shot ("two men in the car"). Any new picture with the men that gets
  a depth map needs people cut-outs and a painted plate, and its depth map must be made from the new
  picture.
- **Scene-level settings.** Rain on the lens (0.6) and the idling-in-rain loop are set per scene
  and need nothing.
- **Wiring.** The extra shots (02, 04, 05, 07, 10, 11) need new `bg` steps in scene 61. Make that
  change through the screenplay build, never by hand-editing `src/data/nobodysWitnessFilm.ts`.
